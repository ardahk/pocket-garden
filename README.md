### Pocket Forest 🌱

Pocket Forest is a **privacy-first** iOS app for daily emotional wellness. All processing happens on-device using Apple Intelligence—your mood data, voice journals, and AI insights never leave your device. Track your emotional patterns, voice-journal your thoughts, and watch a virtual forest grow as you progress.

#### What it does 📝
- **Daily Check-in** - Log how you feel (1–10) and add a short note or voice journal
- **AI-Powered Insights** - On-device transcription with cleanup; friendly Bumblebee feedback after saving
- **Smart Quotes** - Daily/weekly quotes with AFM (iOS 26+) and robust fallback
- **Forest Garden** - Trees grow with your consistency; tap trees to view entries, zoom/pan around your forest
- **Sanctuary Activities** - Guided wellness exercises:
  - **Breathing Exercise** - Calming breathwork 
  - **Muscle Relaxation** - Progressive muscle relaxation  
  - **Gentle Affirmations** - Positive self-talk 
  - **Grounding Technique** - Sensory-based grounding for anxiety
  - **Worry Technique** - Structured worry management practice
  - **Three Good Moments** - Reflective gratitude practice and AI feedback
- **Searchable History** - Browse past entries with tags (mood category, focus area)

#### Tech 🛠️
- SwiftUI, SwiftData, AVFoundation, SwiftWhisper
- Apple Foundation Models (iOS 26+) with fallbacks on older devices

#### Project layout 📁     
- Core/       Design system, models, extensions
- Features/   Home, Journal, Forest, History
- SafeSpace/  Sanctuary activities and wellness exercises
- Services/   Transcription, AFM, quotes, classification
- Components/ Reusable UI
- Sounds/     Audio assets for exercises
- Resources/  Data files (affirmations, etc.)
